FAIR QuickRisk

Rapid quantitative cyber-risk estimation

Quantify your exposure to five common cyber-risk scenarios in under 20 minutes

FAIR QuickRisk asks eight organisation-profile questions and two questions for each of five cyber-risk scenarios. A Bayesian reference-class posterior informed by documented public evidence supplies correlated frequency, materiality and severity parameters. The server then simulates 5,000 years per scenario, drawing Poisson material-event counts and per-event severities. Results show expected annual total loss, posterior-predictive P10, P50 and P90 annual totals and threshold exceedance probabilities.

The five assessed scenarios

  1. Ransomware / destructive extortion
  2. Identity, phishing, social-engineering or business-account compromise
  3. Exploitation of a public-facing vulnerability
  4. Third-party / supplier / managed-service compromise
  5. DDoS / denial-of-service / externally driven service disruption

Methodology in brief

FAIR QuickRisk uses the active FAIR-QK-2.0 posterior-predictive model. Answers modify a reference-class posterior, and simulated years draw material-event counts and event-level severities. Reported P10, P50 and P90 values describe annual total losses under the stated evidence and assumptions; they are not forecasts, guarantees or organisation-specific validation. Shared posterior draws represent the documented dependence model, so the portfolio total has joint percentiles rather than being the sum of independent scenario percentiles. Your data and retention

The QuickRisk questionnaire is zero-retention: your answers and calculated results are processed transiently to produce the assessment. FAIR QuickRisk does not store your answers or results in a database, account, browser storage, cookie or application file. No account or login is required. If you close or refresh the page, the assessment cannot be recovered.

The optional QuickRisk Live overlay is separate: it saves assessments you explicitly choose to save in an anonymous server session, with no name, email or account, and you can archive them or revoke any share link at any time. It uses synthetic SOC data only.

The optional Decision Lab is separate too: if you explicitly save a treatment analysis, it stores the computed comparison, the portfolio search, the treatment selection and the anonymous session in the same database so you can reopen, archive or share the Board Risk Pack. It does not store the questionnaire answers and cannot recompute them from the saved record.
