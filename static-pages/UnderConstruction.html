New features are being built right now

We are actively developing the next set of capabilities for FAIR QuickRisk. Because the application is changing day by day, it is currently open only to colleagues who are taking part in the beta testing programme. As soon as the new work is finished and validated, it will be made available to everyone.

Access while we build

If you are a beta tester, use the access details you were given. Everyone else will be able to use the full application, with no account and no installation, as soon as the beta programme closes. Thank you for your patience.

What is being developed

  • Rapid quantitative assessment

    A short, structured questionnaire that turns answers into loss ranges rather than single figures.

  • Scenario and portfolio insight

    Loss ranges, exceedance probabilities and the drivers that move the estimate the most.

  • Executive and board reporting

    Concise, printable summaries designed for decision makers rather than for specialists.

Beta testing in progress

The beta programme exists so that the methodology, the wording of the questions and the presentation of the results can be tested with real users before general availability. Feedback from beta participants directly shapes what is released. If you would like to take part, please get in touch through the main site.

Findings from the beta are incorporated continuously, which is why the application is not yet open to the public and why parts of it may change without notice.

Coming soon

The completed application will be published here, on the usual address, with full methodology documentation, limitations and disclaimer. No date is announced until the beta review is complete, because the release depends on the outcome of that review.
